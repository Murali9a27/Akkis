Our Story

Born from Love
Baked for Health

Akkis Healthy Baking wasn't created as a business. It began with a son's promise to his parents. After both of Akki's parents became cancer survivors, they had to stop eating refined sugar, maida, and processed foods. Watching them miss their favourite cakes and cookies inspired Akki to start baking healthier alternatives at home. Using whole wheat flour, ragi, organic jaggery, pure desi ghee, and other natural ingredients, he created treats that were both delicious and nourishing. What started as a simple act of love soon became a bakery trusted by families across Hinjawadi. Today, every cake, cookie, and dessert we bake is made with the same care, honesty, and love that inspired our very first recipe. If it's good enough for our parents, it's good enough for every family.

Akki

Founder & Head Baker

Meet the Founder

The son who baked with love

Akki never set out to build a bakery. His journey began with a simple wish to bring joy back to his cancer survivor parents, who could no longer enjoy treats made with refined sugar or maida. Determined to create healthier alternatives without compromising on taste, he spent months experimenting with wholesome ingredients like whole wheat flour, ragi, organic jaggery, and pure desi ghee.

Today, that same passion lives in every product at Akkis Healthy Baking. Every recipe is crafted with the same care, honesty, and love that inspired the very first bake, ensuring every family can enjoy delicious treats made with better ingredients.

"Every recipe begins with one simple thought. If it's good enough for my parents, it's good enough for every family."

— Akki, Founder

From a small home kitchen to a trusted bakery in Hinjawadi, Akki's journey is proof that the most meaningful businesses are often built from love, care, and a desire to make a difference.

What We Stand For

Our values shape every bake

🌾

Whole Ingredients Only

We use whole wheat, ragi, oats, and millets as our foundation — never refined flour. Every grain retains its fibre, minerals, and natural character.

🍯

Sweetness from Nature

Jaggery, organic honey, and seasonal fruit provide sweetness. No refined white sugar ever enters our kitchen — not even in frostings.

🌱

100% Vegetarian

Every single product is vegetarian. We source eggs mindfully and always use plant-based alternatives wherever possible.

🕐

Fresh, Never Frozen

Nothing sits in a freezer overnight. Our batches are baked each morning and delivered the same day — freshness is non-negotiable.

Our Promise

What goes in &
what never does

We believe in full transparency. Here is exactly what you will — and will never — find in anything we bake.

🌾

Whole Wheat & Ragi Flour

Stone-ground, unbleached, full of natural fibre and nutrients.

🍯

Jaggery & Organic Honey

Natural sweeteners with minerals intact — iron-rich jaggery from trusted local suppliers.

🥭

Real Seasonal Fruits & Nuts

Alphonso mangoes, fresh bananas, walnuts, pistachios — never extracts or artificial flavouring.

🫒

Cold-Pressed Oils

We use cold-pressed coconut or sunflower oil — no hydrogenated fats, no margarine.

We never use:

These ingredients have no place in a bakery that truly cares.

Maida (All-Purpose Refined Flour)
Refined White Sugar
Artificial Colours or Flavours
Chemical Preservatives
Hydrogenated Fats or Margarine
High-Fructose Corn Syrup
Artificial Sweeteners (aspartame etc.)
