Our Story

Akki never set out to build a bakery. His journey began with a simple wish — a cake he'd feel genuinely good about giving his own family, without maida or refined sugar hiding inside it.

"If it's good enough for my own kids, it's good enough for every family."

— Akki, Founder

Determined to create healthier alternatives without compromising on taste, he spent months experimenting with whole wheat flour, ragi, organic jaggery and pure desi ghee. Today, that same passion lives in every product we bake.

From a small home kitchen to a trusted bakery in Hinjawadi — the same care, honesty and love goes into every order.
