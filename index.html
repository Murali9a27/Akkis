Why Akkis

Baking that actually cares about you

Every ingredient is chosen for taste and nourishment. No shortcuts, No compromise!

Whole Grain Flours

We use whole wheat and ragi (finger millet) instead of refined flour (maida), keeping fibre, minerals, and flavour intact.

Natural Sweeteners
